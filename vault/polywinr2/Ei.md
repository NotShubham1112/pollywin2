# Ei — Ionisation Energy

Energy required to **remove an electron** from the polymer (related to HOMO level).

- One of the 7 [[Target Properties]] in [[AISEHack 2.0 - Round 2 - MOC|AISEHack 2.0 Round 2]]
- Energetic counterpart of [[Eea]] (electron affinity, related to LUMO level)
- Approximate relation: Egc ≈ Ei − Eea for the chain values — useful for multi-task modeling
- Predicted from [[SMILES]] — see [[Dataset Files]]
