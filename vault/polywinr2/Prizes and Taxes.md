# Prizes and Taxes

Part of: [[AISEHack 2.0 - Round 2 - MOC]] · Source: Rules §9–§10

---

## Prizes

- Prizes are as described on the competition website and only winnable during the stated period.
- Odds of winning depend on number of eligible [[Submission Rules|Submissions]] and participant skill.
- All Prizes subject to **eligibility + rule-compliance review** (see [[Eligibility Rules]]).
- Non-compliant winning Submission → sponsor may:
  1. **Disqualify** the Submission(s), or
  2. Require **remediation within one week** (license conflicts, license obligations, removing violating software)

## Winner Obligations

- Return required prize acceptance documents within **2 weeks** of notification or forfeit (see [[Disqualification and Conduct#Winner Notification]]).
- Prizes awarded ~**30 days** after documents received.
- Prize **transfer/assignment not allowed**.

## Team Prize Split

Monetary Prize split **evenly** between eligible Team members unless the Team unanimously opts for a different split and notifies Kaggle before Prizes are issued. (Team rules: [[Teams and Collaboration Rules]])

## Taxes

> ⚠️ **ALL taxes on Prizes are the sole responsibility of the winners.**

- Winners must submit all requested tax documentation (state, federal, local, foreign).
- Prizes are **net of withheld taxes**.
- Failure to provide documentation → Prize forfeiture → alternate winner selected.
- U.S. resident winners receive **IRS Form-1099**.
