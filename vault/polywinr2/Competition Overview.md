# Competition Overview

Part of: [[AISEHack 2.0 - Round 2 - MOC]]

---

## What is this?

**Round 2** of the Polymer Property Prediction Challenge (AISEHack 2.0). In Round 1, participants predicted two polymer properties; Round 2 is significantly more demanding — models must predict **seven fundamental polymer properties** directly from polymer structures represented as [[SMILES]] strings.

The goal: build **robust and generalizable models** that accurately predict multiple polymer properties from molecular structure.

---

## The 7 Target Properties

See full details in [[Target Properties]]:

| Property | Code | Short Description |
|----------|------|-------------------|
| Chain Bandgap | [[Egc]] | Electronic bandgap of an isolated polymer chain |
| Bulk Bandgap | [[Egb]] | Electronic bandgap of polymer in the bulk phase |
| Ionisation Energy | [[Ei]] | Energy required to remove an electron |
| Electron Affinity | [[Eea]] | Energy released when polymer accepts an electron |
| Dielectric Constant | [[EPS]] | Ability to store electrical energy in an electric field |
| Refractive Index | [[Nc]] | Optical interaction of light with the polymer |
| Glass Transition Temperature | [[Tg]] | Glassy → rubbery transition temperature |
