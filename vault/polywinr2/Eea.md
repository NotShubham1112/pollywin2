# Eea — Electron Affinity

Energy released when the polymer **accepts an electron** (related to LUMO level).

- One of the 7 [[Target Properties]] in [[AISEHack 2.0 - Round 2 - MOC|AISEHack 2.0 Round 2]]
- Energetic counterpart of [[Ei]] (ionisation energy, related to HOMO level)
- Approximate relation: Egc ≈ Ei − Eea — useful for multi-task modeling
- Predicted from [[SMILES]] — see [[Dataset Files]]
