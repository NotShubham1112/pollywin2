# Tg — Glass Transition Temperature

Temperature at which the polymer transitions from a **glassy to a rubbery state**.

- One of the 7 [[Target Properties]] in [[AISEHack 2.0 - Round 2 - MOC|AISEHack 2.0 Round 2]]
- Most distinct from the other targets — a **thermal/mechanical** property rather than electronic/optical
- Strongly influenced by chain flexibility, side groups, and intermolecular forces in the [[SMILES]] structure
- See [[Dataset Files]]
