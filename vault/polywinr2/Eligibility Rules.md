# Eligibility Rules

Part of: [[AISEHack 2.0 - Round 2 - MOC]] · Source: Rules §1–§2 → links to [[Submission Rules]]

---

## To Be Eligible You Must Be

1. A **registered Kaggle account holder**
2. **18+ years old** (or age of majority in your jurisdiction, unless sponsor agrees + parental consent)
3. **Not a resident of**: Crimea, DNR, LNR, Cuba, Iran, or North Korea
4. **Not under U.S. export controls or sanctions** (OFAC lists)

## Company/Employer Entry

- If entering on behalf of a company, institution, or employer, the rules bind **both you and the entity**.
- You warrant your employer knows and consents, including to your potential receipt of a [[Prizes and Taxes|Prize]].

## One Account Rule

- Submissions only under **one unique Kaggle account**.
- Using multiple accounts or a proxy account → [[Disqualification and Conduct|disqualification]].

## Verification

- The sponsor can **verify eligibility and adjudicate disputes at any time**.
- False information (identity, residency, address, phone, email, ownership) → **immediate disqualification**.
