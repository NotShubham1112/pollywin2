## Winner Notification

- Potential winners notified **by email** (see [[Prizes and Taxes]] for next steps).
- A potential winner loses the Prize if they:
  1. Don't respond within **1 week** of first notification attempt, or
  2. Decline within **1 week after Final Submission Deadline**
- An alternate winner is then selected from eligible entries by [[Leaderboard and Evaluation|leaderboard ranking]].
- On request, a potential winner must provide **code and documentation** to verify rule compliance.

## Grounds for Disqualification

- False information during entry → see [[Eligibility Rules]]
- Multiple Kaggle accounts / proxy accounts → see [[Eligibility Rules#One Account Rule]]
- Cheating, deception, or other unfair practices
- Threatening or harassing other Participants, the Sponsor, or Kaggle
- Tampering with the submission process or any part of the Competition Website — this is also a **criminal and civil law violation**; sponsor/Kaggle may seek damages
- Private code/data sharing outside a Team → see [[Teams and Collaboration Rules]]
- Late/void submissions → see [[Submission Rules#Void Submissions]]

## Consequences

- Removal from the leaderboard (Kaggle's discretion)
- Kaggle platform extras (points, medals) may not be awarded
- Final leaderboard is publicly displayed; **sponsor determinations are final and binding**

## Sponsor's Right to Cancel/Modify

If the competition can't run as planned (virus, bugs, tampering, fraud, technical failure...), the sponsor may **cancel, terminate, modify or suspend** it.
